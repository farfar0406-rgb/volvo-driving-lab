    Rules & Controls ⓘ
    +100 Safe Overtake · +50 Close Evasion · +250 Moose Avoided · −180 Vehicle Collision.Hitting any vehicle or the moose ends the run immediately and shows the results. Level up every 2,000 points. LKA simulates steering correction after 2 seconds of lane drift. BLIS simulates correction only when another vehicle is alongside during a lane change; it does not automatically avoid obstacles ahead. AEB is a separate toggle: if you approach a vehicle in the same lane within roughly one car length, simulated emergency braking activates. Stopping does not end the game; you can steer and continue once the road is clear. After 4,000 points, an AI car may overtake from behind and find space to change lanes.

    On mobile, hold the left/right arrows to steer and release to straighten. Use the center controls to brake and accelerate. On desktop, use A / D or arrow keys to steer; S / Down / Space to brake. Sound is optional. Never imitate this game on real roads.
